# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | YTD Sales Analysis

An interactive Tableau dashboard designed to analyze Year-to-Date (YTD) e-commerce performance, tracking key revenue metrics, regional market share, product category growth, and monthly customer acquisition trends compared to the Prior Year (PY).

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to provide stakeholders with a clear, actionable view of global e-commerce performance. By comparing current YTD figures against Prior Year-to-Date (PYTD) benchmarks, the dashboard highlights top-performing regions, identifies profitable vs. underperforming product categories, and tracks shifts in customer acquisition rankings across global markets throughout the year.

---

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

At a high level (with all markets and customer segments selected), the dashboard tracks:

* **Total YTD Sales:** `$14.11M` (`+10,172.68%` vs PY | Latest monthly run-rate: `$0.87M`)
* **Total YTD Profit:** `$1.30M` (`-90.29%` vs PY | Latest monthly run-rate: `$0.09M`)
* **Total Order Quantity:** `157,978` (`+15.02%` vs PY | Latest monthly volume: `8.01K`)

## 🧩 Dashboard Components & Visuals

### 1. Executive KPI Cards with Trend Sparklines
* Displays total **Sales**, **Profit**, and **Order Quantity** alongside percentage variances against the Prior Year (PY).
* Integrated sparklines illustrate monthly trajectories, pinpointing the most recent month's performance (`$0.87M` sales, `$0.09M` profit, `8.01K` orders).

### 2. Regional Market Share (Donut Chart)
* Breaks down the `$14.11M` YTD Sales across five global regions:
  * **LATAM:** `45.86%`
  * **Europe:** `43.37%`
  * **Pacific Asia:** `8.32%`
  * **Africa:** `2.25%`
  * **USCA:** `0.21%`

### 3. Category-wise YTD vs. PYTD Sales (Horizontal Bar Chart)
* Ranks top product categories by YTD revenue and color-codes them based on **Sales Margin vs. PY** (*Greater than PY*, *Less than PY*, or *No Sales in PY*):
  * **Top Revenue Drivers:** Cardio Equipment (`$2.34M`), Fishing (`$1.90M`), Camping & Hiking (`$1.66M`), Water Sports (`$1.34M`), and Cleats (`$1.20M`).
  * **Growth Highlights:** *Camping & Hiking* (`+12.55%` vs PY) and *Water Sports* outperformed prior-year margins, whereas *Cardio Equipment* (`-4.05%`) and *Indoor/Outdoor Games* (`-26.21%`) trailed PY benchmarks. *Computers* (`$0.66M`) represents a new category with no prior-year sales history.

### 4. Market-wise Customer Acquisition per Month (Bump Chart)
* Tracks the monthly ranking (`1` to `5`) of customer acquisition across markets from **January to December**:
  * **LATAM** dominated Rank `#1` from February through May before tapering off in Q3/Q4.
  * **Europe** surged to Rank `#1` during the summer/early autumn peak (July–October).
  * **Pacific Asia** started strong at `#1` in January and reclaimed the `#1` spot in November and December.

### 5. Interactive Filters
* **Market Filter:** Dynamically slice all metrics and charts by specific regions (*All, Africa, Europe, LATAM, Pacific Asia, USCA*).
* **Customer Segment Filter:** Filter performance by customer tier/segment.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. **Revenue vs. Profitability Divergence:** While overall YTD sales and order volume grew substantially compared to PY, total profit saw a sharp decline (`-90.29%`), indicating potential margin compression, heavy discounting, or rising operational/shipping costs that require deeper investigation.
2. **High Regional Concentration:** Nearly **90%** of total revenue is generated by just two regions—**LATAM (`45.86%`)** and **Europe (`43.37%`)**—leaving significant room for expansion in USCA and Africa.
3. **Seasonal Acquisition Shifts:** Customer acquisition leadership rotates predictably across quarters (LATAM in Spring, Europe in Summer/Fall, Pacific Asia in Winter), offering clear timing cues for targeted regional marketing campaigns.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

* **Tableau Desktop / Tableau Public:** Data visualization, dashboard layout, and interactive filtering.
* **Calculated Fields & Table Calculations:** YTD vs. PYTD variance percentages, conditional margin formatting, and monthly `RANK()` calculations for the bump chart.
* **Visual Design:** KPI sparklines, donut charts, diverging/categorical color encoding, and clean dashboard container hierarchy.
